iPhone and Android

Mandata on your phone

Your tasks, deadlines, people and Ask Mandata, in your pocket. Close a task, set a deadline, or send a follow-up you have read first. The phone reaches Mandata on your computer over the Wi-Fi: your mail and the AI stay on the computer.

iPhone and iPad

Scan with the Camera

Opens these steps on the iPhone. Mandata installs on the Home Screen from Safari: nothing to download from the App Store.

  1. On your computer, open Mandata, Settings > Phone, and switch phone access on.
  2. Scan the QR code it shows with the iPhone's Camera, and open it in Safari. The iPhone is paired.
  3. Tap Share , then Add to Home Screen. Mandata now opens full screen from its own icon.
Android

Scan to install the app

Downloads Mandata-Android.apk, the Mandata app for Android 7 and later, built and signed on GitHub from this repository.

Download the Android app
  1. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from your browser: Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back.
  2. On your computer, open Mandata, Settings > Phone, and switch phone access on.
  3. Scan its QR code with the phone's camera and tap Open in the Mandata app. Or open the app and type the address and code shown on the computer.
How it works

The computer does the work

Mandata reads your mail with an AI on your computer. The phone shows what it found and sends back what you change: nothing goes through a server of ours, or anyone else's.

  1. Mandata runs on your computer

    As it does now: it reads Outlook or Microsoft 365, finds the tasks and keeps the profiles. Install it first if you have not.

  2. Phone access is on

    Off until you switch it on in Settings > Phone. Then Mandata also answers on the Wi-Fi, to paired phones only.

  3. The phone is paired once

    With the QR code, or the 8-letter code under it. Each phone gets a key of its own; sign any of them out in Settings > Phone.

If something goes wrong

Questions

The phone cannot reach the computer

Both must be on the same Wi-Fi, and Mandata must be running with phone access on. The first time it is switched on, Windows asks whether Mandata may use the network: allow it on private networks. If you missed it, allow Mandata in Windows Security > Firewall > Allow an app through firewall.

It works at home but not at the office

Office and public Wi-Fi often stop devices on it from reaching each other. Use your home Wi-Fi, or a private network such as Tailscale on both the computer and the phone: Settings > Phone then offers its address for the QR code.

The address changed and the phone lost the computer

Routers sometimes give the computer a new address. Scan the QR code in Settings > Phone again. Your router can also keep the same address for the computer.

Is my mail sent anywhere?

Only between your computer and your phone, on your own network. Nothing goes to a server of ours or of anyone else. On the network the connection is not encrypted, so use networks you trust, as you would for a shared folder.

Android will not install the app

Allow your browser to install apps: Settings > Apps > Special app access > Install unknown apps, choose the browser, turn on Allow from this source. Then open the downloaded file again. Some work phones do not allow apps from outside their store at all; use Mandata in Chrome instead (scan the QR code in Settings > Phone, then Chrome's menu > Add to Home screen).

Why is it not in the App Store or Google Play?

Mandata is an innovation sandbox project. On iPhone, the Home Screen app from Safari works without a store; on Android, the app installs from this page.

How do I remove it?

On the phone, More > Sign out this phone, then remove the app as any other. On the computer, sign it out or switch phone access off in Settings > Phone.
