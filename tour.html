Why Mandata works the way it does

Four rules sit behind every screen in the tour. They are why you can trust the list without checking your inbox again.

Tasks come from email

Nobody types tasks in. Each one is read from the email that asked for it and closes when the email that settles it arrives.

Deadlines are never guessed

A task has a deadline only when an email sets one, quoted word for word, or when you set it yourself.

Nothing is sent without you

The AI drafts follow-ups and forwards, but an email leaves only when you press Send and confirm.

Private by design

The AI runs on your own computer through Ollama. Your email and your tasks never leave it.
